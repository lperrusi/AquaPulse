# Privacy Policy – AquaPulse

**Last updated:** February 2026

This privacy policy describes how AquaPulse ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

---

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account data:** When you register or sign in (email/password or Google), we store your email, display name, and profile data (e.g. age, weight, activity level, gender) to personalize your hydration goal and experience.
- **Hydration data:** Your daily water intake, goals, reminders, and streaks are stored locally on your device and, if you use an account, may be synced to our servers (Firebase) so you can use the app across devices.
- **Device and usage data:** We may collect analytics (e.g. Firebase Analytics) such as app opens, feature usage, and general device type to improve the app. We do not sell this data.
- **Location (optional):** If you enable weather-based recommendations, we use your location to fetch local weather. Location is not stored on our servers beyond what is needed for the weather request.
- **Ads:** If the app shows ads (e.g. Google AdMob), ad providers may collect identifiers and usage data according to their own policies. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 2. How we use your information

- To provide and personalize the app (goals, reminders, stats).
- To sync your data across devices when you use an account.
- To improve the app and fix issues (analytics, crash reports).
- To show relevant ads when the app is ad-supported.

We do not sell your personal data.

---

## 3. Data storage and security

- Account and synced data are stored using Google Firebase. We use industry-standard security practices.
- Local data on your device is stored in the app’s private storage.

---

## 4. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate data (e.g. via the Profile screen).
- **Delete** your account and associated data (e.g. via in-app account deletion or by contacting us).
- **Export** your data (we can provide it in a readable format on request).

To exercise these rights or ask questions, contact us at the support address below.

---

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses:

- **Firebase** (Google): authentication, database, analytics. [Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Sign-In:** for account sign-in. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **AdMob** (if applicable): ads. [Google Ads Privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- **OpenWeatherMap** (if weather is enabled): weather data. [OpenWeatherMap Privacy](https://openweathermap.org/privacy)

Each has its own privacy policy; we encourage you to read them.

---

## 6. Children

AquaPulse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it.
